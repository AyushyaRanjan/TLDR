# Why saved articles go unread

People save a long article because the title promises a result, then never
return. The cost is not the file size. It is the time to rebuild context:
what the piece claims, which terms are new, and whether it answers the
question they had on the day they saved it.

A useful first pass is short. Sixty to ninety words can say the claim and the
outcome. A second pass of a few hundred words can explain the mechanism and
define the jargon. The detailed notes stay tied to pages so a reader can jump
back when a sentence looks wrong.

This sample is intentionally short so a local 3B model can finish it in one
or two calls during an eval.
